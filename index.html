Method and sources

Odds come from 300,000 simulated seasons of the rest of Week 12 through Week 17 under the league's seeding rules, run Fri Nov 27 at about 8:20 PM ET with Week 12 scores through Friday's game and rosters as of 8:20 PM ET. Clinch conditions come from an exact check of all 64 win/loss outcomes of each division's remaining games, with points tiebreaks treated as open.

  • Tiebreaks used: division titles go by record, then head-to-head among the tied teams, then total points. Seeds 1-3 (the division winners) go by record, then total points. Seeds 4-5 go by record, then total points. Seed 6 goes to the remaining team with the most points.
  • Week 12: each team's live ESPN score plus ESPN's projection for its current starters yet to play. The spread is 19 points per team for a full week, scaled by the square root of the share of starters still to play.
  • Player projections (Weeks 13-17): the average of three sources. DraftSharks supplies per-game point projections (the mean of its own projection and the consensus projection it lists). FantasyPros' consensus rest-of-season rankings and 4for4's rest-of-season rankings (set to this league's scoring) publish ranks, not points, so each rank is converted to points using the DraftSharks per-game projection at the same rank in its position, ordered by projection. Players expected to miss games use DraftSharks only, because their rankings already reflect the missed time, and are treated as missing the first games of the stretch; expected missed games come from the number of remaining games DraftSharks projects for each player.
  • Playoff-week matchups (Weeks 15-17): each player's projection is multiplied by the square root of his opponent's 4for4 schedule-adjusted fantasy points allowed at his position, divided by the league average. The square root applies the matchup at half weight, because season-long projections already account for part of each schedule.
  • Team scores (Weeks 13-17): each team's best available lineup (QB, RB, RB/WR, three WR, TE, K), with byes and expected injury absences applied and any empty slot filled at waiver-wire level (the best unrostered player's DraftSharks projection at that position), scaled to this league's average score (x1.232) and blended 65/35 with the team's actual scoring average. Weekly swings of 20.8 points per team are measured from this season's scores before Week 12.
  • Players without a projection: rostered players none of the three services projects are treated as out: Terrance Ferguson (Molesters), Aaron Rodgers (Black Lodge) and Alec Pierce (Vince's Pizza).
  • Assumptions: every owner starts their best lineup from Week 13 on. The #1 seed is assumed to pick the weaker-projected Week 16 opponent.
