Method and sources

Odds come from 300,000 simulated seasons of Weeks 12-17 under the league's seeding rules, run Tue Nov 24 with all Week 11 results. Clinch conditions come from an exact check of all 64 win/loss outcomes of each division's remaining games, with points tiebreaks treated as open.

  • Tiebreaks used: division titles go by record, then head-to-head among the tied teams, then total points. Seeds 1-3 (the division winners) go by record, then total points. Seeds 4-5 go by record, then total points. Seed 6 goes to the remaining team with the most points.
  • Week 12: each team's best lineup by ESPN's Week 12 projections in league scoring, as of Tue Nov 24, with a standard deviation of 19 points per team.
  • Player projections (Weeks 13-17): the average of three sources. DraftSharks supplies per-game point projections (the mean of its own projection and the consensus projection it lists). FantasyPros' consensus rest-of-season rankings and 4for4's rest-of-season rankings (set to this league's scoring) publish ranks, not points, so each rank is converted to points using the DraftSharks projection of the player at the same positional rank. Players expected to miss games use DraftSharks only, because their rankings already reflect the missed time, and are treated as missing the first games of the stretch.
  • Playoff-week matchups (Weeks 15-17): each player's projection is multiplied by the square root of his opponent's 4for4 schedule-adjusted fantasy points allowed at his position, divided by the league average. The square root applies the matchup at half weight, because season-long projections already account for part of each schedule.
  • Team scores (Weeks 13-17): each team's best available lineup, with byes and expected injury absences applied and any empty slot filled at waiver-wire level, scaled to this league's scoring (x1.063) and blended 65/35 with the team's actual scoring average. Weekly swings of 20.8 points per team are measured from this season's scores through Week 11.
  • Players without a projection: rostered players none of the three services projects are treated as out if injured or listed by ESPN as not on an NFL team (Puka Nacua, Alec Pierce, Brandon Aiyuk and others), or as low-end depth if healthy.
  • Assumptions: every owner starts their best lineup from Week 12 on. The #1 seed is assumed to pick the weaker-projected Week 16 opponent.
